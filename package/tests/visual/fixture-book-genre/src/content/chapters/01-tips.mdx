# Tips

This chapter exercises the `<Tip>` numbered component + the `<TipsCard>` print-friendly back-matter card. Both back the cross-volume tip-registry pattern from the Pragmatic Programmer precedent ([#70](https://github.com/brandon-behring/book-scaffold-astro/issues/70)).

## A first tip

<Tip n="1" title="Care About Your Craft">
  Why spend your life developing software unless you care about doing it well?
</Tip>

Tips are referenced by number from later chapters: see Tip 1 above.

## A second tip

<Tip n="2" title="Think About Your Work">
  Turn off the autopilot and take control. Constantly critique and appraise your work.
</Tip>
