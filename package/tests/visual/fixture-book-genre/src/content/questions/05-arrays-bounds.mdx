What happens when you read **past the end** of an array?

<Rationale appendix for="q-arrays-bounds">
Bounds behavior is a language contract, not a universal: Java/Python raise
(`ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException` / `IndexError`), JavaScript yields
`undefined`, and C/C++ make it undefined behavior. Exam answers that say
"always" are the trap options. (v4.21.0 #114: this rationale renders as an
appendix link in the bank and in full on /answers.)
</Rationale>
