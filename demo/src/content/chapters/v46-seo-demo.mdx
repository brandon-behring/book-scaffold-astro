# v4.6 SEO demo

This chapter exists to exercise v4.6's article meta-tag surface end-to-end. When the demo builds, the page should emit:

- `<meta property="og:type" content="article">` (from Chapter.astro passing `ogType="article"`)
- `<meta property="article:author" content="Brandon Behring">` (from frontmatter `author`)
- `<meta property="article:published_time" content="2026-05-26T...">` (from `published`)
- `<meta property="article:modified_time" content="2026-05-27T...">` (from `updated`)
- `<meta property="article:tag" content="seo">` + 3 more (one per `tags[]` item)

Plus the baseline 11 SEO tags every page gets from Base.astro: canonical, og:title/description/url/type/site_name, twitter:card/title/description, plus og:image/twitter:image (default `/og-default.png` from `defineBookConfig.seo.ogImage`), plus twitter:site (`@brandonbehring`).

## Why a separate chapter?

The other demo chapters (`test-labels.mdx`, `_example-chapter.mdx`, `_week01-hello-world.mdx`) predate v4.6 and have minimal frontmatter. Rather than retrofit them with `author`/`published`/`updated`/`tags` (which would conflate "demo content" with "feature flag"), this chapter is the dedicated v4.6 exemplar.

Future scaffold improvements that touch the chapter meta-tag surface should bump this chapter's frontmatter accordingly (e.g., if v4.7 adds JSON-LD structured data, add fields here that exercise it).
